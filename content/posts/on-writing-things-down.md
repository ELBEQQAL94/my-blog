---
title: On Writing Things Down
date: 2026-08-15
description: A short note on why keeping a public log of ideas is worth the effort.
tags: [meta, writing]
---

Writing something down, even badly, forces a level of clarity that thinking
alone rarely produces. Half-formed ideas fall apart the moment you try to put
them into sentences, and the ones that survive are usually worth keeping.

That's really the only reason this blog exists.
